Decision problem

Quality decisions require integrated statistical evidence across capability, measurement systems, experimentation, risk, and defect behavior.

The product is built as an evidence chain: diagnose the measurement system, quantify process capability, detect instability, test hypotheses, and only then recommend an intervention.
