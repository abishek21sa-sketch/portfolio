System logic

How the work is structured.

01

Estimate

Build market/risk inputs from public financial data.

02

Optimize

Compare minimum-variance, mean-variance, CVaR, Black-Litterman, and LP-CVaR allocations.

03

Detect regime

Use a three-state Gaussian HMM to condition analysis on changing market states.

04

Stress

Use Monte Carlo paths and VaR/CVaR views to examine downside behavior.
